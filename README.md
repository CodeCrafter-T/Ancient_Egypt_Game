# Ancient_Egypt_Game

This is a text based, single-player, immersive game,coded entirely in C, that transports you to the world of ancient Egypt by blending interactive storytelling ,trategic combat and clever puzzles.

#### " You are the 'Chosen One', destined to save your kingdom after mysteriously surviving a sacrificial ritual. Your quest is to acquire four elemental powers by navigating battlefields, solving ancient puzzles, and defeating mythical beasts. Your journey culminates in a descent into the underworld to confront the ultimate evil threatening the land."
